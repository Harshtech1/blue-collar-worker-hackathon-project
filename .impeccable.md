## Design Context

### Users
RAHI is used by operations admins, founders, and investor-facing stakeholders who need to steer a live blue-collar marketplace across Agra zones. Their job is to understand density, workforce mix, proof-of-work quality, and business burn fast enough to make tactical deployment decisions in real time.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, precise, and locally empowered. The interface should feel like a war-room command surface for a serious marketplace business, not a static analytics poster or a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a financial-grade command-center tone with RAHI Indigo, slate, emerald, and controlled amber for interventions. Prefer structured, route-aware operational surfaces, geospatial control panels, and briefing-style intelligence modules over decorative cards. Avoid black-box visuals, placeholder-looking AI UI, and bland enterprise gray.

### Design Principles
1. Every analytics surface should drive an operational decision, not just display a metric.
2. Zone context must stay visible so the admin always knows which geography the system is talking about.
3. High-stakes states should feel confident and legible, with dense information but strong hierarchy.
4. Intelligence features should read like a real business operating system: map, model, strategy, and intervention in one flow.
5. Visual polish should support trust and seriousness without feeling cold, generic, or over-decorated.
